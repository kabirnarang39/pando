Questions

What is an active agent?

An agent account (agent/NAME or bot/NAME) that lands at least one change in your organisation during a calendar month: a land, a compare-and-swap that moves a branch, or a change request it wrote or landed. Refused lands don't count, and people are never agents. An agent that lands nothing that month costs nothing.

Is there a limit on lands?

Fair use is {{FAIR_USE_LANDS}} lands per paid agent per month. Going past it starts a conversation with us, never an automatic charge.

What happens when we reach a plan limit?

A new agent's land is refused with a message saying so; agents already active that month keep landing. Over the storage limit, writes are refused and reads keep working. Adding a person beyond the plan is refused. Nothing is ever deleted.

Is single sign-on extra?

No. OpenID Connect sign-in (Okta, Entra ID, Google, Auth0, Keycloak) is included in every tier, and in the open-source server.

Where is {{NAME}} Cloud available?

India first: Cloud is for customers in India, billed in rupees with GST. Outside India, self-hosted support is available today by invoice, and you can join the waitlist for Cloud in your region.

What if {{NAME}} goes away?

The server is Apache-2.0 and runs on your own machines, and pando export-git writes your history back to git with identical trees, any time.
